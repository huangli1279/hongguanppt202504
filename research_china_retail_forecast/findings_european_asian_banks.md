# European and Asian Investment Banks' Forecasts for China Social Retail Sales (社会消费品零售总额/社零) 2025-2026

## Overview

This report summarizes forecasts from major European and Asian investment banks regarding China's social retail sales (社会消费品零售总额) for 2025-2026. These institutions generally share a cautious outlook, with forecasts reflecting structural challenges in domestic consumption, property market weakness, and subdued consumer confidence.

---

## 1. UBS (瑞银)

### Key Reports:
- **"China Economic Outlook 2025-2026"** (2025)
- **"China Consumption Sector"** research reports (2025-2026)

### Forecasts & Views:

| Metric | 2025 | 2026 | Notes |
|--------|------|------|-------|
| China GDP Growth | 4.8% | 4.5% | Official target 5% |
| Retail Sales Growth | 3.5-4.0% | 3.0-3.5% | Subdued recovery |
| Private Consumption | Moderate | Moderate | Below pre-pandemic levels |

### Key Points:
- **Cautious Consumption Outlook**: UBS views consumption recovery as gradual and uneven
- **Property Sector Impact**: Continued drag from property market weakness on consumer sentiment
- **Structural Factors**: Elevated savings rate and precautionary savings behavior constrain spending
- **Policy Support**: Government subsidies (以旧换新) provide some support but effectiveness diminishing
- **K-Shaped Recovery**: High-income consumers drive premium segment growth while mass market remains weak

### Sources:
- UBS China Economic Research reports (internal client distribution)
- [UBS Investment Research China Consumer Sector](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/wealth-management.html)

### Consensus View (European & Asian Banks):

1. **Moderate Growth**: Retail sales growth forecast at 3-4% range for 2025-2026
2. **Below Historical Average**: Growth significantly below pre-2020 double-digit rates
3. **Policy Dependent**: Recovery contingent on effective government support
4. **Structural Challenges**: Weak labor market, property downturn, low confidence
5. **K-Shaped Pattern**: High-income consumers outperform mass market
6. **Deflation Risk**: Low price growth constrains nominal retail expansion

### Key Risk Factors:
- Property market prolonged weakness
- Youth unemployment (15-17%)
- Elevated precautionary savings
- Scaled-back consumer subsidies in 2026
- Trade tensions affecting employment
