# US Investment Banks' Forecasts for China Social Retail Sales (社会消费品零售总额/社零) 2025-2026

## Overview

This report summarizes forecasts from major US investment banks regarding China's social retail sales (社会消费品零售总额) for 2025-2026. The forecasts reflect a cautious outlook with consensus pointing to moderate growth amid structural challenges in domestic consumption.

---

## Morgan Stanley (摩根士丹利)

### Key Reports:
- **"Luxury Goods Market Outlook 2026: From Contraction to Caution"** (2026)
- **"China Consumer: Where is consumption trending now"** (June 2025)
- **"China Musings: Beijing Will Fine-tune, Not Pivot"** (June 2025)

### Forecasts & Views:

| Metric | Forecast |
|--------|----------|
| China GDP Growth 2025 | 4.8% |
| China GDP Growth 2026 | ~4.5% |
| Personal Luxury Goods Growth 2026 | ~2.5% (down from earlier 4-5% estimate) |

### Key Points:
- **Structural Challenges**: Morgan Stanley characterizes China's consumption environment as facing "structural and cyclical challenges rather than sustained expansion"
- **Era of Double-Digit Growth Over**: "The era of consistent double-digit expansion is likely over" - particularly for younger and middle-income consumers
- **2026 Luxury Outlook**: Downgraded from earlier forecast of 4-5% to 2.5% growth for personal luxury goods sector
- **K-Shaped Economy**: Benefits high-income consumers while aspirational shoppers show increased caution
- **Consumer Concerns**: High unemployment, AI impact on job prospects, property downturn affecting confidence
- **2026Q GDP Tracking**: Q2 GDP tracking around 4.4%, with risk of approaching lower end of 4.5-5% target range

## Goldman Sachs (高盛)

### Key Reports:
- **"Leaning Against the Wind"** - 2025 China Economic Outlook (November 2024)
- **"China's Economy Expected to Grow 4.8% in 2026"** (2026)
- **"China Consumer Dashboard 2026Q2"** (July 2026)

### Forecasts & Views:

| Metric | 2025 Estimate | 2026 Forecast |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| China GDP Growth | 5.0% (official target met) | 4.8% |
| Household Real Income Growth | 5.0% YoY | Moderate, slowing |
| Government Consumption Growth | - | 5.0% |
| Household Consumption vs GDP | Outpace overall GDP | Outpace overall GDP |

### Key Points:
- **Consumption Theme 2025**: Focus on domestic consumer and real estate markets; growth impetus shifting from exports to policy support
- **2026 Outlook**: Household consumption growth expected to moderate, but government consumption acceleration to offset weakness
- **Policy Support**: Large-scale localized debt plans and expansive fiscal policies expected to support consumption
- **Service Consumption**: Expected to outpace goods consumption growth
- **Labor Market**: Weak labor market constrains household spending; urban surveyed unemployment at 5.1%
- **Consumer Confidence**: Low consumer confidence and declining house prices negatively impact spending
- **2026Q2 Data**: 
  - Household disposable income per capita: 5.6% YoY (nominal)
  - Household savings rate: 32.6%
  - Real retail sales growth decelerated significantly in Q2

## JP Morgan (摩根大通/J.P. Morgan)

### Key Reports:
- **"2026年中国经济展望"** (2025年末/2026年初)
- **"2026年亚洲展望"** - Private Bank Asia

### Forecasts & Views:

| Metric | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| China Retail Sales Growth | 3.7% (actual) | **2.6%** | **2.5%** |
| China GDP Growth | - | 4.3% (range: 4.1-4.6%) | 4.1% |
| Urban Unemployment | ~5% | ~5% | - |
| Youth Unemployment | 14-18% | 14-18% | - |

### Key Points:
- **Moderate Growth Outlook**: 2026 and 2027 retail sales growth expected to maintain "moderate" levels at 2.6% and 2.5%
- **Structural Challenges**: Constrained by weak labor market, slowing income growth, and property market downturn
- **Policy Support**: Old-for-new consumer goods subsidies (家电以旧换新) to continue but may be "optimized" (scaled down)
- **Consumption Sector Rating**: China consumption stocks have become attractive after 5 years of underperformance (2021-2025)
- **2025 Nov Data**: Social retail sales YoY only 1.3% in November, continuing to slow from Q1-Q3
- **Profit Outlook**: 2025 industry sales growth expected at 3.7%, net profit growth at 8.8% (may be revised down 2-3% without further stimulus)
- **Investment Focus**: 
  1. Home appliances and gold jewelry leaders benefiting from premiumization
  2. IP derivatives, beverages, cosmetics aligned with "affordable self-reward" trends
  3. Sports apparel and home appliance leaders with overseas expansion capability

## Summary Comparison

| Bank | 2025 Retail Sales | 2026 Retail Sales | 2027 Retail Sales | Outlook |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Morgan Stanley** | - | Structural challenges; luxury 2.5% | - | Cautious |
| **Goldman Sachs** | Income +5% | Consumption to moderate | - | Neutral |
| **JP Morgan** | 3.7% (actual) | **2.6%** | **2.5%** | Cautious |

### Consensus View:
1. **Moderate Growth**: All banks forecast single-digit retail growth for 2025-2027
2. **Structural Headwinds**: Weak labor market, property downturn, low consumer confidence
3. **Policy Dependence**: Consumption reliant on government support policies
4. **K-Shaped Recovery**: High-income consumers outperforming middle/lower income groups
5. **Service vs Goods**: Service consumption growth expected to outpace goods retail

### Key Risk Factors:
- Subsidy tapering (补贴退潮)
- Export growth slowdown
- AI-related job displacement concerns
- Prolonged property market weakness
- Deflationary pressures (PPI negative for extended period)
