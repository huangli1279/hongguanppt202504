# Research Plan: Past Industrial Revolutions Employment Impact

## Main Research Question
What are the historical facts and data about the three-phase employment impact (displacement → complementarity → creation) across the First, Second, and Third Industrial Revolutions?

## Subtopics

### Subtopic 1: First Industrial Revolution (1760s-1840s)
- Employment structure changes in British textile industry
- Child labor, factory system emergence
- Luddite movement timing and impact
- Net employment trend (total jobs before/after)
- Timeframe for each phase

### Subtopic 2: Second Industrial Revolution (1870s-1914)
- Electrification and American manufacturing employment
- Rise of white-collar/clerical jobs
- Fordism and mass production employment effects
- Overall employment growth rate
- Timeframe for each phase

### Subtopic 3: Third Industrial Revolution / Internet Age (1970s-2010s)
- Computerization and job polarization in the US
- Frey & Osborne / Oxford study on automation risk percentages
- Service sector employment growth
- China factory employment peak and decline
- Timeframe for each phase

## Synthesis
Combine findings into a comparative table showing each revolution's three-phase employment effects with approximate timelines and key metrics.
